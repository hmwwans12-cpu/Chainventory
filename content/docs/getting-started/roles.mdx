## Overview

Chainventory uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with five roles. Each role has specific permissions for inventory, members, and blockchain operations.

## Roles

### Owner

The warehouse creator. Has full access to everything.

- **Can:** All operations (inventory, members, blockchain, settings)
- **Can assign:** Manager, Staff, Auditor, Viewer
- **Special:** Cannot be removed by anyone (including themselves via the UI)

### Manager

A senior team member who can manage inventory and members.

- **Can:** Create/edit/archive products, stock in/out/adjustment/reversal, manage members
- **Can assign:** Staff, Auditor, Viewer
- **Cannot:** Manage other Managers or Owners, suspend warehouse, transfer ownership

### Staff

A team member who can perform day-to-day inventory operations.

- **Can:** Create products, stock in/out, view inventory and blockchain
- **Cannot:** Adjust/reverse stock, manage members, approve join requests

### Auditor

A read-only role for checking inventory and blockchain records.

- **Can:** View inventory, movements, blockchain records, export products
- **Cannot:** Make any changes to inventory or members

### Viewer

The most restricted role. Read-only access to basic inventory data.

- **Can:** View inventory, movements, blockchain records
- **Cannot:** Export data, manage members, make any changes

## Role Assignment Rules

- **Owner** can assign any role.
- **Manager** can assign Staff, Auditor, and Viewer — but **never** another Manager or Owner.
- A Manager **cannot** manage another Manager's permissions.
- Role changes require the actor to have `member:manage` permission **and** the target role must be in their assignable list.

## Join Requests

When you invite someone to your warehouse:

1. A join request is created with status `PENDING`.
2. An Owner or Manager must approve it.
3. Once approved, the member's status becomes `ACTIVE`.
4. The new member can then access the warehouse according to their assigned role.

## Suspended Members

Members can be suspended by Owners or Managers. Suspended members:

- Cannot access the warehouse
- Their existing sessions are invalidated
- They can be reactivated by an Owner or Manager
