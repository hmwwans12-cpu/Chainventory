## Warehouse Code

Every warehouse has a unique **warehouse code** — a short identifier used to invite team members. When you create a warehouse, a code is generated automatically. Share this code with team members who need to join.

## Stock Movement Types

Chainventory supports four types of stock movements:

### Stock In

Increases the balance of a product. Used when receiving new inventory from suppliers.

- **Permission required:** `stock:in`
- **Effect:** Balance increases by the specified quantity

### Stock Out

Decreases the balance of a product. Used when shipping or selling inventory.

- **Permission required:** `stock:out`
- **Effect:** Balance decreases by the specified quantity
- **Validation:** Cannot exceed current balance (prevents negative stock)

### Adjustment

Corrects a stock count to match a physical audit. Can be positive or negative.

- **Permission required:** `stock:adjustment`
- **Requires approval:** Owner or Manager must approve adjustments
- **Effect:** Balance is set to the new value

### Reversal

Reverses a previous stock movement (partial or full). Used to correct mistakes.

- **Permission required:** `stock:reversal`
- **Requires approval:** Owner or Manager must approve reversals
- **Validation:** Cannot reverse more than the original movement quantity

## Blockchain Verification

Every important stock movement (Stock In, Stock Out, Adjustment, Reversal) creates a **proof** — a cryptographic record anchored to the blockchain. This provides:

- **Integrity:** Records cannot be silently altered after the fact.
- **Audit trail:** Every change is traceable and verifiable.
- **Transparency:** Anyone can verify a record on Base Sepolia.

Learn more at [Blockchain & Verification](/docs/getting-started/blockchain).

## Real-Time Updates

Stock balances update in real-time across all connected clients. When a team member performs a stock movement, everyone sees the updated balance immediately — no refresh needed.

## Inactivity Policy

To keep the system healthy, warehouses that have no stock movements for 27 days are **suspended**. Suspended warehouses:

- Cannot process new stock movements
- Members cannot be added or removed
- The warehouse can be reactivated by performing any stock movement

This policy ensures inactive warehouses don't consume resources unnecessarily.
