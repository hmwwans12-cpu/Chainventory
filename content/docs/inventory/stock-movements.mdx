## Overview

A **stock movement** is any change to a product's balance. Every movement is timestamped, attributed to the member who performed it, and (for important types) anchored to the blockchain as a proof. Movements are the heart of your inventory audit trail.

## Movement Types

| Type       | Effect on balance | Requires approval | Permission        |
| ---------- | ----------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Stock In   | Increases         | No                | `stock:in`        |
| Stock Out  | Decreases         | No                | `stock:out`       |
| Adjustment | Set to new value  | Owner/Manager     | `stock:adjustment`|
| Reversal   | Undoes a movement | Owner/Manager     | `stock:reversal`  |

See [Core Concepts](/docs/getting-started/concepts) for a detailed description of each type and its validation rules.

## Performing a Movement

1. Go to **Inventory > Movements**.
2. Click **Stock In** or **Stock Out**.
3. Select the product and enter the quantity.
4. Add an optional note (e.g. supplier name or order reference).
5. Submit. The balance updates in real-time for everyone.

<Callout type="warning">
  Stock Out validates against the live balance. If the balance changed since you
  opened the form, the submission fails with **Stale Stock** — refresh and
  re-enter the quantity.
</Callout>

## Transfers

Chainventory models transfers as a paired Stock Out from one product context and a Stock In to another. Because each warehouse currently holds a single inventory scope, transfers are represented as two linked movements sharing a `transferId`. This keeps both sides independently verifiable on-chain.

## Audit Trail

Every movement records:

- The product and quantity
- The type and direction
- The performing member
- A timestamp (UTC)
- An optional note
- A linked proof ID once anchored

You can open any movement to see its full detail and jump to its blockchain proof.

## Filtering & Search

The movements list supports filters by:

- Product (or SKU)
- Movement type
- Date range
- Member

Combine filters to investigate a specific discrepancy quickly.

## Approvals

Adjustments and reversals require an Owner or Manager to approve before they affect the balance. Pending approvals show a **Pending** badge and can be approved or rejected from the movement detail view.

<Callout type="info">
  Rejected adjustments leave the original balance untouched and are recorded as a
  rejected action in the audit trail for transparency.
</Callout>
