## Overview

Products are the core entities you track in Chainventory. Each product represents a physical or digital item held in your warehouse. Products are shared across all members of a warehouse and are referenced by every stock movement.

## Creating a Product

1. Navigate to **Inventory > Products**.
2. Click **Add Product**.
3. Fill in the required fields:

   - **Name** — a human-readable label (e.g. `Wireless Mouse`).
   - **SKU** — a unique stock-keeping unit. This must be unique within the warehouse.
   - **Unit** — the unit of measure (`pcs`, `kg`, `box`, etc.).
   - **Category** — an optional grouping used for filtering and reports.

4. Click **Save**.

<Callout type="info">
  SKUs are enforced for uniqueness at the warehouse level. If you reuse a SKU,
  the save will fail and you'll be prompted to choose another.
</Callout>

## Editing a Product

Open a product from the list and click **Edit**. You can change the name, unit, category, and stock settings. Note that changing the unit after movements exist does **not** retroactively convert historical quantities — it only affects future movements.

## Stock Levels & Thresholds

Each product tracks:

- **Current Balance** — the live quantity computed from all stock movements.
- **Low-Stock Threshold** — a number that, when the balance drops at or below it, triggers a low-stock notification.

Set a threshold under the product's **Stock Settings** tab. For example, a threshold of `10` on an item that sells fast ensures you get warned before you run out.

## Categories

Categories are free-form labels that help you group products. They are used for:

- Filtering the product list
- Grouping analytics reports
- Exporting subsets of inventory

You can create a category inline while editing a product — there is no separate category management screen.

## Archiving

When a product is discontinued, archive it instead of deleting it. Archived products:

- Are hidden from the default product list (toggle **Show archived** to view)
- Keep their full movement history
- Cannot receive new stock movements

Archiving preserves the audit trail while keeping the active list clean.

## Example Product Object

A product is represented internally roughly as:

```json
{
  "id": "prod_8f2c...",
  "sku": "WM-001",
  "name": "Wireless Mouse",
  "unit": "pcs",
  "category": "Peripherals",
  "lowStockThreshold": 10,
  "archived": false,
  "balance": 142
}
```

## Permissions

| Action                | Who can do it            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| View products         | All roles                |
| Create/edit products  | Owner, Manager, Staff    |
| Archive products      | Owner, Manager           |
