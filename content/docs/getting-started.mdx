## Overview

Chainventory is modern inventory management with real-time stock tracking and blockchain verification. This guide walks you through your first steps.

## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Visit [chainventory.vercel.app/signup](https://chainventory.vercel.app/signup).
2. Sign up with your email or connect a wallet via Privy.
3. An embedded wallet is created automatically — you don't need to set up MetaMask or any external wallet to get started.

## Step 2: Create a Warehouse

1. After sign-up, you'll be prompted to create your first warehouse.
2. Enter a warehouse name and select the type (Retail, Warehouse, Office, etc.).
3. Click **Create Warehouse**.
4. A blockchain transaction is submitted to deploy your warehouse contract on Base Sepolia (a free test network).

<Callout type="info">
  Creating a warehouse deploys a smart contract on Base Sepolia. This is a test
  network — no real money is involved.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Add Your First Product

1. Navigate to **Inventory > Products**.
2. Click **Add Product** and fill in the product name, SKU, and unit.
3. Your product is now ready for stock movements.

## Step 4: Perform Stock In

1. Go to **Inventory > Movements**.
2. Click **Stock In**.
3. Select the product, enter the quantity, and add an optional note.
4. Submit — the stock balance updates in real-time.

## Step 5: Invite Team Members

1. Navigate to **Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member** and enter their email.
3. Choose a role (Manager, Staff, Auditor, or Viewer).
4. The invited member receives a join request to approve.

## Step 6: Join an Existing Warehouse

If you received a warehouse code:

1. Go to **Join Warehouse**.
2. Enter the warehouse code shared by your owner/manager.
3. Your join request is sent for approval.
