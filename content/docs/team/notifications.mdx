## Overview

Chainventory keeps you informed about inventory events through in-app notifications. They are delivered in real-time and surfaced via the bell icon in the top bar.

## Notification Types

- **Low-Stock Alert** — a product dropped to or below its threshold.
- **Join Request** — someone requested to join your warehouse (Owners/Managers).
- **Join Approved/Rejected** — the outcome of your own request.
- **Approval Required** — an Adjustment or Reversal is waiting for your approval.
- **Movement Recorded** — a team member performed a stock movement (configurable).
- **Proof Confirmed** — a movement's blockchain proof reached Confirmed status.
- **Warehouse Suspended** — your warehouse hit the 27-day inactivity limit.

## Read / Unread State

Notifications start as **unread** and show a badge count on the bell icon. Clicking a notification marks it read and (where relevant) navigates to the related record. You can mark all as read from the notification panel.

## Real-Time Delivery

Notifications use Supabase Realtime. When an event happens, connected clients receive it within seconds — no polling or refresh required. If your tab was backgrounded or disconnected, missed notifications are delivered on reconnect and reconciled against the server's record.

<Callout type="info">
  If real-time updates stop appearing, refresh the page to re-establish the
  Realtime connection. Background browser tabs may be throttled by the browser.
</Callout>

## Preferences

Owners and Managers can tune which notification types are enabled from **Settings > Notifications**. Per-member muting of non-critical alerts (like generic movement recordings) helps reduce noise on busy warehouses.

## Email Digests

Critical events (low-stock, suspension, join requests) are also sent by email so you're covered even when you're not logged in. Routine movement notifications are in-app only unless you opt into email digests.

## Permissions

All roles receive notifications relevant to their access. Auditors and Viewers, for example, won't get "Approval Required" notices since they cannot approve.
