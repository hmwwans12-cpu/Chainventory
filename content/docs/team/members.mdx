## Overview

A warehouse is a shared workspace. You invite people by email, assign them a role, and they join after approval. Membership is managed from the **Members** page.

## Inviting a Member

1. Go to **Members**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter the invitee's email.
4. Choose a role (Manager, Staff, Auditor, or Viewer).
5. Click **Send Invite**.

The invitee receives an email with a link. When they accept, a **join request** is created with status `PENDING`.

## Join Requests

Every new member must be approved before they can access the warehouse:

1. A request is created with status `PENDING`.
2. An **Owner** or **Manager** reviews and approves (or rejects) it.
3. On approval, the member's status becomes `ACTIVE`.
4. The member can now access the warehouse per their role.

<Callout type="warning">
  Managers can approve join requests, but they can only assign roles within
  their own assignable set (Staff, Auditor, Viewer) — never another Manager or
  Owner.
</Callout>

## Assigning & Changing Roles

Open a member and click **Change Role**. The same rules from [Roles & Permissions](/docs/getting-started/roles) apply:

- **Owner** can assign any role.
- **Manager** can assign Staff, Auditor, Viewer only.
- Role changes take effect immediately and are recorded in the audit log.

## Removing a Member

Owners and Managers can remove a member. Removed members lose access instantly and their sessions are invalidated. An Owner cannot be removed through the UI.

## Suspending a Member

Instead of removing, you can **suspend** a member:

- They cannot access the warehouse.
- Their existing sessions are invalidated.
- They can be reactivated later by an Owner or Manager.

Suspension is useful for temporary leaves or offboarding without losing history.

## Membership Limits

There is no hard cap on member count, but each account can belong to multiple warehouses via invites. Remember that each account can only **create** one warehouse of their own (see the FAQ).
