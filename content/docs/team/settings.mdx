## Overview

Workspace Settings is where you control identity and connection details for your warehouse and account. Access it from the user menu in the top-right corner.

## Profile

- **Display Name** — shown to other members.
- **Avatar** — optional image upload.
- **Locale / Timezone** — used for date displays and report ranges (UTC-based storage, localized display).

## Wallet

Chainventory creates an **embedded wallet** for you automatically via Privy when you sign up — you never have to install MetaMask. In Settings > Wallet you can:

- View your embedded wallet address.
- See the network it operates on (**Base Sepolia**).
- Disconnect external wallet connections if you linked one.

<Callout type="info">
  Private keys for the treasury and embedded wallets never leave the server.
  Daily inventory work does not require you to hold or understand crypto.
</Callout>

## Account

- **Email** — used for invites and critical notifications. Keep it up to date.
- **Password / Auth** — managed through the auth provider; reset via the login screen.
- **Sessions** — view active sessions and sign out remotely if needed.

## Warehouse Settings (Owner only)

The warehouse owner can manage warehouse-level settings:

- **Warehouse name and type** — Retail, Warehouse, Office, etc.
- **Warehouse code** — regenerate to invalidate old invite links.
- **Suspend / Reactivate** — manual suspension (separate from the inactivity policy).
- **Transfer ownership** — hand the warehouse to another active member.

## Notifications Preferences

Located under Settings > Notifications (see [Notifications](/docs/team/notifications)) — toggle which event types reach you by email or in-app.

## Danger Zone

Owners can transfer ownership here. After transfer, your role becomes the role assigned by the new owner (commonly Manager). Ownership transfer is irreversible from the UI and is recorded in the audit trail.
