## Overview

In Chainventory, a **transaction** refers to the on-chain submission that anchors a stock movement's proof to the blockchain. Transactions are not payments — they are integrity records. No money moves; only a hash of your movement data is written to your warehouse contract on Base Sepolia.

## How a Transaction Is Created

1. You perform a stock movement (Stock In, Stock Out, Adjustment, or Reversal).
2. The system computes a hash of the movement payload and creates a **proof** with a unique `proofId`.
3. The proof enters the submission pipeline and is sent to your warehouse smart contract.
4. The resulting transaction receives a `txHash` once broadcast.

## Linking Movements to Transactions

Each important movement carries a `proofId`. The proof stores the `txHash` once the transaction is confirmed. This creates a verifiable chain:

```
Stock Movement → Proof (hash) → Blockchain Transaction (txHash)
```

You can navigate from a movement directly to its proof, and from the proof to BaseScan using the transaction hash.

## Transaction Lifecycle

| State         | Meaning                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Pending       | Proof created, queued for submission             |
| Submitted     | Transaction broadcast, awaiting confirmations    |
| Confirmed     | ≥2 confirmations on Base Sepolia                 |
| Failed        | Submission error — can be retried from Developer |
| Manual Review | Flagged for operator attention                   |

## Verifying a Transaction

1. Open the movement and click its proof.
2. Copy the `txHash`.
3. Visit [sepolia.basescan.org](https://sepolia.basescan.org) and search the hash.
4. Confirm the contract address matches your warehouse contract and the block timestamp is reasonable.

<Callout type="tip">
  Bookmark your warehouse contract address from the Blockchain page — verifying
  any future proof is then a one-step search on BaseScan.
</Callout>

## What Is Stored On-Chain

Only the **hash** of the movement data is stored on-chain. The plaintext payload (product, quantity, performer) stays in the database. This means:

- The blockchain proves the record existed and was not altered.
- No sensitive business data is publicly exposed.

## Gas & Costs

Transactions are paid for by the warehouse **treasury** signer using test ETH from the faucet. There is no cost to you, and Base Sepolia ETH has no real-world value.
