## Overview

The Analytics section summarizes your warehouse activity so you can spot trends, identify fast movers, and catch stock risks before they become problems.

## Available Metrics

- **Total Products** — count of active products.
- **Current Stock Value (units)** — sum of balances across products.
- **Stock In / Stock Out volume** — quantities moved in and out over a period.
- **Net Change** — Stock In minus Stock Out.
- **Low-Stock Count** — number of products at or below their threshold.
- **Movements Over Time** — a daily or hourly timeline of all movement types.

## Period Ranges

Pick a window to scope every chart and table:

- **Today**
- **Last 7 days**
- **Last 30 days**
- **Last 90 days**
- **Custom range** — choose a start and end date (UTC).

Changing the range updates all widgets instantly. Comparisons ("vs previous period") are shown as a percentage delta where applicable.

## Filtering by Category

Most reports can be filtered by product category, letting you analyze a single line of business without exporting raw data.

## Low-Stock Report

The low-stock widget lists every product at or below its configured threshold, sorted by urgency (most negative margin first). Use it as a replenishment checklist.

## Export

Owners and Managers can export analytics as CSV:

1. Open the report you want.
2. Click **Export CSV**.
3. The file downloads with the current filters and date range applied.

<Callout type="info">
  Exports respect your selected period and category filters. Sensitive payload
  data is never included — only aggregated metrics and product identifiers.
</Callout>

## Realtime vs Snapshot

Analytics charts reflect real-time balances, but historical timelines are built from the immutable movement log. Because movements are blockchain-anchored, your historical reports are tamper-evident: a reconciled number today will match a reconciled number a year from now.
