## Overview

This page covers operational and setup problems beyond the FAQ. For end-user questions, see [Troubleshooting & FAQ](/docs/help/faq).

## Wallet / Signup Issues

**Embedded wallet not created**
- Signup via Privy should auto-provision an embedded wallet. If not, sign out and sign back in.
- Check the Developer Console → Dependencies for auth provider health.

**Cannot connect external wallet**
- Ensure the wallet supports Base Sepolia, or simply use the embedded wallet — it requires no setup.

## Blockchain Submission Issues

**Proof stuck in Pending**
- The pipeline may be busy or the RPC is down. Wait a few minutes; proofs process automatically.
- Check Developer Console → Dependencies for pipeline and RPC status.

**Proof Failed (gas)**
- The treasury is out of test ETH. Claim from the faucet (Owner/Manager) in Developer Console → Treasury.
- Then manually retry the failed proof from the console.

**Proof Failed (contract error)**
- The warehouse contract may be paused or the signer removed from the allowlist. Verify the allowlist in Developer Console.

<Callout type="warning">
  Never remove the active treasury signer from the allowlist without adding a
  replacement first — this blocks all future proof submissions.
</Callout>

## Movement Errors

**Insufficient Stock on Stock Out**
- Reduce the quantity to the available balance, or perform a Stock In first.

**Stale Stock on Stock Out**
- The balance changed since the form opened. Refresh and re-enter the quantity.

**Adjustment / Reversal blocked**
- These require Owner or Manager approval. Ask an approver to review the pending request.

## Connectivity

**Real-time updates not working**
- Refresh to re-establish the Supabase Realtime connection.
- Check your network; background tabs may be throttled by the browser.

**Page won't load / 500 errors**
- Check Developer Console → Dependencies for downstream outages (database, RPC, QStash).
- If the warehouse was suspended for inactivity, perform any stock movement to reactivate.

## Data Verification

**Can't find a proof's transaction**
- Open the movement → proof → copy the `txHash` → search on [sepolia.basescan.org](https://sepolia.basescan.org).
- Confirmed proofs always have a txHash; Pending/Failed ones do not yet.

## Getting Further Help

If the steps above don't resolve the issue, capture the relevant proof ID or movement ID and share it with your warehouse Owner, who can inspect the Developer Console for details.
